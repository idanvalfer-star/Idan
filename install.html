Install Cartly 1 tap

Your browser can install this for you.

On iPhone & iPad Safari

Apple doesn't let a website install itself, so this part is manual — it takes about five seconds.

  1. Tap Share at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add — the Cartly icon appears on your home screen.

On Android Chrome

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu, top-right in Chrome.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  3. Confirm with Install.

On your computer Chrome / Edge

  1. Look for the install icon at the right of the address bar.
  2. Click it, then click Install.
  3. Cartly opens in its own window from then on.
